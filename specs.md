Timro jiwan
Timro Jiwan is a digital medical identity app that gives every person their own Health ID, similar to a national ID but focused entirely on healthcare. The app securely stores and connects important medical information such as blood group, allergies, medications, prescriptions, vaccination records, lab reports, previous illnesses, and hospital visits. When a person visits a different hospital, clinic, or laboratory, they can use their Timro Jiwan Health ID to securely access or share their medical history instead of carrying physical documents or explaining their entire medical history again. Users remain in control of their information and can choose which healthcare provider can access their records and for how long. In an emergency, authorized medical staff could also access limited critical information such as allergies, blood group, medications, and emergency contacts. The long-term goal of Timro Jiwan is to connect hospitals, clinics, laboratories, and pharmacies across Nepal so that a person's medical history can follow them wherever they receive healthcare.
